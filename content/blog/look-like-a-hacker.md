Not every "hacking" session needs an exploit. Sometimes you just need green text
and a straight face. This is a joke tutorial, so keep your expectations at
zero and your drama at maximum.

> Warning: the only thing you will compromise here is your friends' sense of
> reality.

## The routine

**1. Open Command Prompt.**

**2. Turn the screen "hacker green":**

```text
color a
```

Instant movie-hacker upgrade. The text goes green and your credibility goes up
three levels.

**3. Make the terminal scroll dramatically:**

```text
dir /s
```

The screen fills with fast-scrolling text as it lists every file and folder
recursively. Looks intense. Is not.

**4. Stare at the screen.** Seriously. Furrow the brow.

**5. Occasionally mutter:**

> "Hmm... something's off with this system."

**6. If someone asks what you are doing:**

> "Just checking the directory structure."

**7. Do not explain further.** Mystery is part of the professionalism.

## What is actually happening?

No hacking whatsoever. `color a` just changes the Command Prompt text color to
green, and `dir /s` simply lists the files in a folder along with its subfolders.
But to someone who does not know, it already looks like you are breaking into the
Pentagon. 😭

## The tiny real lesson

Looking like hacking is not hacking. Both commands here are read-only and
completely harmless. Real security work is the opposite of this bit: it is
methodical, documented, and always authorized. For the version with actual
substance, the other posts in [Field Notes](/blog) are a better use of your green
text.

## Aksinya

**1. Buka Command Prompt.**

**2. Ubah layar jadi "hijau hacker":**

```text
color a
```

Upgrade instan jadi hacker film. Teksnya hijau, kredibilitasmu naik tiga level.

**3. Bikin terminal scroll dramatis:**

```text
dir /s
```

Layar penuh teks berjalan cepat karena menampilkan semua file dan folder secara
rekursif. Kelihatan intens. Padahal tidak.

**4. Tatap layar.** Serius. Kerutkan dahi.

**5. Sesekali bergumam:**

> "Hmm... ada yang aneh di sistemnya."

**6. Kalau ada yang bertanya sedang apa:**

> "Lagi cek struktur direktorinya."

**7. Jangan jelaskan lebih lanjut.** Misteri adalah bagian dari profesionalisme.

## Apa yang sebenarnya terjadi?

Tidak ada proses hacking sama sekali. `color a` cuma mengubah warna teks Command
Prompt jadi hijau, dan `dir /s` cuma menampilkan daftar file dari sebuah folder
beserta subfoldernya. Tapi bagi orang yang tidak tahu, tampilannya sudah cukup
seperti sedang membobol Pentagon. 😭

## Pelajaran kecil yang beneran

Terlihat seperti hacking bukan berarti hacking. Kedua perintah di sini bersifat
read-only dan sepenuhnya aman. Kerja keamanan yang sesungguhnya justru kebalikan
dari lelucon ini: metodis, terdokumentasi, dan selalu berizin. Untuk versi yang
ada isinya, post lain di [Field Notes](/blog) jauh lebih layak menemani teks
hijaumu.
